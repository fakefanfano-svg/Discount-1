About CrochetSimply

CrochetSimply is an independent digital magazine dedicated to modern fiber crafts: free step-by-step crochet patterns, stitch conversion charts, yarn calculators, and photo galleries of finished work from makers around the world.

Editorial Standards

Physical gauge testing: every pattern we publish is physically swatched and stress-tested with genuine yarn samples before release.
No synthetic shortcuts: pattern instructions and yarn yardage figures are calculated according to Craft Yarn Council (CYC) standards, not generated automatically.
